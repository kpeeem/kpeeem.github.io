Claudometer is an ESP32-S3 board with a 400×300 three-colour e-paper panel. It shows the same limits Agent Meter shows in the menu bar: the Claude 5-hour window, the week, Fable and Codex. The Mac sends the numbers over Bluetooth LE, so the board holds no tokens at all, and the screen does not glow and barely uses power.

One row per limit, and the set is picked in Agent Meter or in the setup portal. cached instead of a countdown means the reading is older than an hour or its window has already reset. The header shows what the agents are doing: working, done, failed or “needs you” with the project name.

Fig. 4Three rows: bigger numbers, nothing extra.

Fig. 5The second page; the USER button flips pages.

The board is a RockBase NM-EPD-420: ESP32-S3R8, 16 MB of flash, e-paper and two buttons. A full refresh of the three-colour panel takes about 10 seconds. The firmware is built with PlatformIO on GxEPD2, U8g2, NimBLE-Arduino and ArduinoJson and updates over Wi-Fi. A printable case lives in the repository.

The screenshots are drawn by the real firmware code: ui.cpp also builds on a Mac with thin Arduino stubs.

Source, firmware and case: github.com/kpeeem/claudometer
