DitherPhil opens photos and videos from Photos or Files. Change the algorithm, palette and cell size in the bottom panel while the frame stays on screen. Tap the comparison button to see the original.

Source →

Fig. 1

Bayer comes in three matrix sizes. Atkinson, Floyd–Steinberg, Sierra Lite and stippling give the frame a less regular texture. Keep the source colors, pick a palette or set the shadow and highlight colors yourself. Exposure, contrast, saturation and warmth are applied before dithering.

Core Image and Metal process the frame on the device. DitherPhil uses a smaller frame for preview and renders the effect again at the export resolution. PNG keeps transparency, while JPEG and HEIC offer a quality control. Video exports to MP4 with HEVC or H.264 and the source audio.

The editor is written in SwiftUI and runs on iOS and iPadOS 26 or later.

Version 1.1 opens from the system share sheet, loops video while you edit and saves named presets. Photo export starts rendering immediately. Save the output to Photos, or use the Dither media action in Shortcuts to process a file with a preset.
