DiskSpot shows what fills the disk: a map, a tree with shares, file kinds and a cleanup tab. It first takes big files and packages from the Spotlight index, then walks the blind spots exactly with getattrlistbulk — the whole data volume, nine million files, in about two minutes.

Download >

Fig. 1

Snapshots and comparisons show what grew since last time. The cleanup tab gathers caches, logs, DerivedData and package caches that DiskSpot measures itself, together with advice from Claude.

Fig. 2

Through an MCP server Claude measures the disk with disk_recent, disk_top and disk_system and sends every finding with a verdict and a reason straight into the app. The agent deletes nothing on its own: paths go to the Trash only after you confirm, and commands come from a closed list.

Source and build: github.com/kpeeem/DiskSpot
